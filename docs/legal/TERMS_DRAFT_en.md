1. **What the service is.** A tool that, when you start a scan, tries to identify a clip
   playing in another app on the same phone by comparing it with a limited catalogue on the
   phone. [Counsel: describe the catalogue actually shipped; L0 builds contain synthetic test
   material only and must say so.]
2. **Limited catalogue.** Only titles in the installed catalogue can be recognised. A "no
   confident match" result does not mean a clip is unknown or unlicensed. Results marked
   "possible" are not confirmed.
3. **No guarantee of accuracy.** [Counsel: wording.]
4. **Your responsibilities.** Use the app only on content you are allowed to view; do not use
   it to capture private communications or other people's information. [Counsel: wording.]
5. **What the app does not do.** It does not download, record or share the video, does not
   bypass content protection, and does not capture without your action. [Engineering fact;
   counsel to confirm phrasing.]
6. **Privacy.** See the privacy notice.
7. **Changes.** A new version, including a translation change, will be shown and must be
   accepted before scanning continues.
